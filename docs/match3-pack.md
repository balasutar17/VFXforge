# Match-3 effect pack (0.51)

Twenty-two effects for match-3 games in four new library categories, plus a
**Chain link** shape. A tile is taken as about 1.2 units across, as for the
older Match-3 effects. Every effect grades Light or OK on the phone budget.

**Gem Clears.** Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple and Orange Gem Clear: a white
flash, a ring, gem shards and facets in the gem's colour, and twinkles. One
builder, six colours, so they read as one set on the board.

**Match-3 Specials.**
- Cross Blast: two striped pieces swapped, beams along the row and the column.
- Area Blast: a wrapped piece clearing the 3 x 3 around it (square shock, chunks, smoke).
- Colour Bomb: a swirling orb zapping lightning to the pieces of one colour, then a rainbow burst.
- Special Piece Made: sparkles drawn in, a ping and a swirl as a special piece forms.
- Swap Shine: a quick glint as two pieces swap.
- No Match: small grey puffs as a swap that makes no match bumps back.
- Collect to Goal: a piece flying along a curve to the goal counter (top right), and a pop there. Move the curve's end to where your counter is.
- Board Twinkle: a slow, looping sparkle over the board while the player thinks.

**Combos.** Combo Good, Great and Amazing: the same rays, flash and stars,
bigger each step; Great adds a ring, Amazing gold rays and confetti.

**Blockers.** Ice Crack, Chain Snap (uses the new Chain link shape), Crate
Break, Chocolate Splat and Honey Burst.
